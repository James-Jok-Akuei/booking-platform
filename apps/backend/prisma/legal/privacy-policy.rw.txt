2. AMAKURU DUKUSANYA

- Amaduka ubika nk’ayo ukunda.

2.2 Amakuru ashyirwaho iyo ukoresha porogaramu

- Inyandiko z’ubwishyu: umubare w’amafaranga, igihe, nimero ya Mobile Money yakoreshejwe, na nimero iranga ubwishyu itangwa n’ikigo gitanga serivisi yo kwishyura. Ntitwigera twakira PIN yawe ya Mobile Money.
- Kuba waremeye Amabwiriza y’Imikoreshereze yacu n’iyi Politiki y’Ibanga, hamwe n’itariki na verisiyo.
- Amamenyesha twakoherereje n’uko wayafunguye cyangwa utayafunguye.
- Inyandiko za tekiniki zibikwa na seriveri zacu, nk’igihe ubusabe bwakiriwe na raporo z’amakosa, zikoreshwa mu kurinda umutekano wa serivisi no gutuma ikomeza gukora.

2.3 Aho uherereye

Niba ubyemeye, porogaramu ikoresha aho telefoni yawe iherereye kugira ngo yerekane intera iri hagati yawe n’amaduka, ku murongo ugororotse no mu muhanda, kandi iyatondeke hakurikijwe intera. Aho uri hakoreshwa muri ako kanya kugira ngo hasubizwe ibyo ushakisha. Ntituhabika kuri konti yawe kandi ntidukurikirana aho ujya. Kugira ngo tubare intera yo mu muhanda, aho uri n’aho iduka riri byoherezwa kuri serivisi ishakisha inzira, itakira izina ryawe cyangwa imeyili yawe.

“Promosiyo za buri cyumweru kuri imeyili” zo ziratandukanye, kuko zoherezwa igihe udakoresha porogaramu. Kuri zo, ushobora guhitamo akarere ku rutonde, kwandika aderesi ukayihitamo mu hantu tubonye, cyangwa gukanda “Koresha aho ndi ubu”, bifata aho telefoni yawe iri muri ako kanya nk’akadomo ku ikarita (urugero, iwawe mu rugo). Tubika ako kadomo kamwe hamwe n’izina ry’umuhanda kariho. Ako kadomo tugakoresha gusa mu guhitamo serivisi ziri hafi yako zijya muri imeyili ya buri cyumweru no gupima intera yazo mu muhanda, kandi imeyili ivuga aderesi yoherejwe ku bwayo. Amaduka ntajya akabona. Aho uri muri ako kanya ntihajya hakoreshwa kuri izi imeyili. Ushobora guhindura aderesi cyangwa gukanda “Siba” igihe icyo ari cyo cyose, kandi isibwa iyo ufunze konti yawe.

2.4 Ibisubizo by’ibanga, nk’ibijyanye n’ubuzima

Amaduka amwe abaza ikibazo kijyanye n’ubuzima bwawe cyangwa ikindi kintu cy’ibanga kugira ngo ashobore kuguha serivisi mu buryo butekanye: allergie ku biryo, gutwita, imvune umuvuzi agomba kumenya, ubwoko bw’ubufasha mu by’amategeko ukeneye. Ni wowe uhitamo gusubiza cyangwa kudasubiza. Ibi bisubizo ni amakuru bwite yihariye, kandi tubyitwararika kurusha ibindi byose:

- Tubikoresha gusa mu kubiha iduka uri gufatiraho gahunda. Gusubiza ni uruhushya rweruye utanga kuri ibyo, kandi ushobora guhindura cyangwa gukuraho igisubizo muri porogaramu kugeza amasaha 2 mbere y’uruzinduko.
- Bibikwa bifungiranye (bihishwe mu buryo bw’ibanga), bitandukanijwe n’ibindi bigize gahunda yawe.
- Ni wowe n’iryo duka gusa mushobora kubibona. Ntibijya bishyirwa muri imeyili, mu imenyesha rya telefoni cyangwa mu butumire bwo muri kalendari.
- Itsinda ryacu ry’ubufasha rishobora kubisoma gusa iyo wowe cyangwa iduka mudusabye ubufasha kuri iyo gahunda. Buri gihe bibaye byandikwa hamwe n’impamvu, kandi gahunda yerekana ko itsinda ryacu ry’ubufasha ryabisomye.
- Tubisiba iminsi 30 nyuma y’uruzinduko.

Ntitujya tugusaba ibyavuye mu isuzuma rya muganga, amateka y’ubuzima bwawe, ibisubizo by’ibizamini cyangwa imiti ufata.

2.5 Ibyo tudakusanya

Ntidukusanya nimero y’indangamuntu yawe, iya pasiporo cyangwa iy’ubwishingizi, nimero z’abantu ubitse muri telefoni yawe, cyangwa PIN yawe ya Mobile Money. Ntidukoresha ibikoresho bikurikirana abantu ku bw’amatangazo yamamaza, kandi ntitugurisha amakuru yawe.


3. IMPAMVU DUKORESHA AMAKURU YAWE, N’ISHINGIRO RYACU MU MATEGEKO

Itegeko ryo Kurengera Amakuru Bwite ritwemerera gukoresha amakuru bwite gusa iyo hari ishingiro ryemewe n’amategeko. Amashingiro yacu ni aya:

- Gushyira mu bikorwa amasezerano dufitanye nawe. Gufungura konti yawe, kwakira gahunda, gucunga avansi n’amafaranga asubizwa, kohereza ubutumwa bwemeza gahunda na kode zo kugera, kugushoboza kwandikira iduka ubutumwa, no gusubiza ubusabe bwawe bw’ubufasha.
- Kubahiriza amategeko. Kubika inyandiko z’ibaruramari n’iz’imisoro, gusubiza ibyo inzego zibifitiye ububasha zisaba mu buryo bwemewe n’amategeko, no gukemura ibirego by’abaguzi.
- Ku bw’inyungu zacu zemewe, igihe zitaruta uburenganzira bwawe. Kurinda umutekano w’Urubuga, gukumira uburiganya n’imikoreshereze mibi, gukosora amakosa, kwerekana ibitekerezo, no kumva muri rusange uko Urubuga rukoreshwa kugira ngo turunoze.
- Ku bw’uruhushya rwawe. Gukoresha aho telefoni yawe iherereye, kukoherereza “Promosiyo za buri cyumweru kuri imeyili”, no kwerekana ifoto yawe ya porofayili. Ushobora gukuraho uruhushya watanze igihe icyo ari cyo cyose, kandi ibyo ntibizagira ingaruka ku byakozwe mbere.
- Ku bw’uruhushya rwawe rweruye. Guha iduka ufatiraho gahunda ibisubizo byawe ku bibazo by’ibanga, nk’ibijyanye n’ubuzima (reba 2.4).

11. COOKIES N’IBINDI BIKORESHO BISA NAZO

Ntidukoresha cookies zo kwamamaza, cookies zikurikirana abantu cyangwa ibikoresho bisesengura imikoreshereze — haba muri porogaramu, haba no ku mbuga zacu za interineti (urupapuro rwacu rw’ibanze, porogaramu y’abacuruzi n’aho itsinda ryacu rikorera akazi). Ni yo mpamvu utigera usabwa kwemera cyangwa kwanga cookies: nta n’imwe ikeneye ko uhitamo.

Ibibikwa ku gikoresho cyawe ni ibyo serivisi ikeneye gusa kugira ngo ikore:

- Kwinjira kwawe. Porogaramu ya telefoni ibika amakuru yabyo ahantu harinzwe muri telefoni yawe. Ku mbuga zacu za interineti abikwa mu bubiko bwa mushakisha yawe kuri mudasobwa yawe. Gusohoka birayasiba.
- Amahitamo mato wakoze, nk’igice (tab) wafunguye cyangwa inama wafunze. Aguma muri mushakisha yawe cyangwa muri telefoni yawe kandi ntiyoherezwa ku muntu n’umwe.
- Amamenyesha. Niba uyemeye, telefoni yawe cyangwa mushakisha yawe iduha aderesi yo kuyoherezaho. Kuzimya amamenyesha, cyangwa gusohoka kuri iyo telefoni, biyikuraho.

Imbuga zacu za interineti zikura inyuguti zazo (fonts) kuri seriveri zacu bwite, aho kuzikura ku z’ikindi kigo. Amakarita ari ku mbuga zacu za interineti ashushanywa na OpenStreetMap: iyo ikarita igaragaye, mushakisha yawe isaba seriveri zabo amashusho y’ikarita, bityo babona aderesi yawe ya interineti — ntibabona izina ryawe cyangwa ikindi cyose kikwerekeyeho. Ibyerekana inzira bifungukira muri porogaramu y’amakarita cyangwa urubuga rwa interineti wihitiyemo.

Nitwigera twongeraho ikintu kitari itegeko, nko kubara umubare w’abantu basura urupapuro, tuzabanza tukubaze, kandi uzashobora kubyanga.
