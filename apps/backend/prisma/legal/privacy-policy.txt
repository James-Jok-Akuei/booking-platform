2. THE DATA WE COLLECT

2.1 Data you give us

- Your email address, which is how you sign in.
- A display name and a profile picture, if you choose to add them.
- The mobile money numbers you use to pay, and any you save for next time.
- Your bookings: the business, the service, how many, the date and time.
- The choices you make when you book, such as your hair length or the size of your car, and any note you write to the business.
- Messages you send to businesses, and photos you attach to them.
- Requests you send to our support team, and files you attach.
- Reviews and ratings you write.
- The businesses you save as favourites.
- Your district, or the address you save, if you choose one for "Weekly offers by email".

2.2 Data created when you use the app

- Payment records: the amount, the time, the mobile money number used, and the reference given by the payment provider. We never receive your mobile money PIN.
- Your acceptance of our Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy, with the date and the version.
- Notifications we have sent you and whether you have opened them.
- Technical records kept by our servers, such as the time of a request and error reports, used to keep the service secure and working.

2.3 Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to show how far businesses are from you, by straight line and by road, and to sort them by distance. Your position is used at that moment to answer the search. We do not save it to your account and we do not track where you go. To work out road distances, your position and the business's are sent to a route-finding service, which does not receive your name or email.

"Weekly offers by email" is different, because it is sent when you are not using the app. For it you can choose a district from a list, type an address and pick it from the places we find, or press "Use where I am now", which takes the point where your phone is at that moment (for example your home). We save that one point with its street name. We use it only to choose services near it for the weekly email and to measure their distance by road, and the email says which address it was sent for. It is never shown to businesses. Your live location is never used for these emails. You can change the address or press "Forget it" at any time, and it is deleted when you close your account.

2.4 Private answers, such as health

Some businesses ask a question that touches on your health or another private matter so that they can serve you safely: a food allergy, a pregnancy, an injury a therapist should know about, the kind of legal help you need. Answering is up to you. These answers are sensitive personal data, and we handle them more strictly than anything else:

- We use them only to give them to the business you are booking with. Answering is your explicit consent to that, and you can change or remove an answer in the app until 2 hours before the visit.
- They are stored locked (encrypted), apart from the rest of your booking.
- Only you and that business can see them. They are never put in an email, a phone notification or a calendar invite.
- Our support team can read them only when you or the business ask us for help with that booking. Each time is recorded with the reason, and the booking shows that our support team read them.
- We delete them 30 days after the visit.

We never ask you for a diagnosis, your medical history, test results or the medicines you take.

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect your national ID, passport or insurance numbers, your contacts, or your mobile money PIN. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your data.


3. WHY WE USE YOUR DATA, AND OUR LEGAL BASIS

The Data Protection Law allows us to use personal data only where there is a lawful basis. Ours are:

- To perform our contract with you. Creating your account, taking bookings, handling deposits and refunds, sending confirmations and arrival codes, letting you message a business, and answering your support requests.
- To comply with the law. Keeping accounting and tax records, answering lawful requests from authorities, and handling consumer complaints.
- For our legitimate interests, where these do not override your rights. Keeping the Platform secure, preventing fraud and abuse, fixing faults, showing reviews, and understanding in general terms how the Platform is used so we can improve it.
- With your consent. Using your phone's location, sending you "Weekly offers by email", and showing your profile picture. You can withdraw consent at any time, and it will not affect what was done before.
- With your explicit consent. Passing your answers to private questions, such as health, to the business you book with (see 2.4).

7. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR DATA

- Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections.
- We sign you in with one-time codes sent to your email, so there is no password of yours for anyone to steal from us.
- Sign-in codes are stored in protected form, not as readable text.
- Answers to private questions, such as health, are stored locked (encrypted) and kept apart from the rest of a booking.
- Only staff who need your data to do their work can access it.
- Our support team does not read your messages with a business. They read the messages about one booking only when you or the business ask us for help with that booking, or a problem with it is reported to us. Each time is recorded, and the booking shows that our support team read the conversation.
- When our support team acts on your behalf — checking you in, cancelling a booking you asked us to cancel, signing you out of a lost phone — it is recorded with the reason, and you are told what was done.
- Payments are approved on your own phone. A saved mobile money number cannot be charged without that approval.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of personal data happens that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the NCSA within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, as the Data Protection Law requires, and we will tell you without delay where the law requires that too.


8. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the Data Protection Law you have the right to:

- be informed about how your data is used, which is the purpose of this policy;
- access your data and get a copy of it;
- have data corrected if it is wrong or out of date;
- have data erased, where we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it;
- object to our use of your data, including for marketing;
- ask us to restrict how we use your data while a question about it is resolved;
- receive your data in a form you can move to another service (portability);
- not be subject to a decision made only by automated means that seriously affects you. We do not make such decisions;
- withdraw your consent at any time;
- name a person who may exercise these rights for you, including after your death.

How to use these rights in the app:

- Get a copy of your data: Account, then "Get a copy of my data".
- Correct your name or picture: Account.
- Stop marketing emails: turn off "Weekly offers by email" in Account, or use the unsubscribe link in any such email.
- Stop location use: turn off location for the app in your phone's settings.
- Close your account and remove your identity: Account, then "Close my account".

For anything else, write to jokditakeerleek@gmail.com. We do not charge for this. We will answer within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm that you are the owner of the account before we act.


9. CHILDREN

The Platform is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data, tell us and we will remove it.


10. MARKETING

We send marketing emails only to people who have turned on "Weekly offers by email". These emails are sent at most once a week, are marked as offers, and each one has a link to unsubscribe with one tap. Emails about your own bookings are not marketing and are always sent.


11. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TOOLS

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or analytics — not in the app, and not on our websites (our home page, the business app and our team's console). That is why you are never asked to accept or refuse cookies: there are none that need your choice.

What is kept on your device is only what the service needs to work:

- Your sign-in. The phone app keeps it in your phone's secure storage. On our websites it is kept in your browser's storage on your computer. Signing out removes it.
- Small settings you chose, such as a tab you opened or a tip you closed. They stay in your browser or phone and are not sent to anyone.
- Notifications. If you allow them, your phone or browser gives us an address to send them to. Turning notifications off, or signing out of that phone, removes it.

Our websites load their letters (fonts) from our own servers, not from another company's. Maps on our websites are drawn from OpenStreetMap: when a map shows, your browser asks their servers for the map pictures, so they see your internet address — not your name or anything else about you. Directions open in a maps app or website you choose.

If we ever add something optional, such as counting how many people visit a page, we will ask you first, and you will be able to say no.
